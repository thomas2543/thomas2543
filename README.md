Gamified charity crowdfunding where every donation becomes distance to run, ride, swim or walk, verified by Strava or GPS. https://thesweatdebt.com

### Baraka Maps

iOS app mapping masjids, prayer spaces and Islamic events, used by thousands. Not currently available. https://barakamaps.com

### Rate That Crypto

iOS and Android game-style app for rating crypto projects, used by thousands. I worked alongside the team. Not currently available.

### Pay site network

Front Line Pay, UK Teacher Pay, Prison Officer Pay and Firefighter Pay, built on the Police Pay model.
